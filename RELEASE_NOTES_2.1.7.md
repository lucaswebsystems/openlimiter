# OpenLimiter 2.1.7

Every Claude bar comes from one source. The direct request to Anthropic is off until you turn on Read my Claude usage from Anthropic in Connect Tools, and nothing asks you to. While it is off, Claude bars come from Claude Code's status line; while it is on, every Claude bar comes from that request, about every five minutes, and a failed request says so beside the bars it leaves.

The first run connects your tools first, asks nothing about Claude and keeps Show my bars on screen. A tool that is set up says Waiting for first reading until it reads, a tool that needs its sign in keeps its row on Usage, and the Codex sign in counts down with Try again.

Drag a provider by its grip, or use its move menu, to reorder providers in the window and the edge panel; the tray menu follows, and Settings, Order switches between Most used first and My order. The edge panel grows to fit its rows when the screen has room.

Theme presets work in light and dark: Graphite, Meadow and terminal captions are free, and Ember is the one Pro preset. The status line keeps to its width on one row and ends with +N more when the readings do not fit.

On the macOS preview, the app reads the login shell's PATH and looks in the Homebrew, Volta, Bun and pnpm folders, and a newer version links to its download page instead of saying Update check unavailable. On Linux without a tray, closing the window quits the app.

The site lists what Pro adds in one place, offers both plans, shows the paired phone its real plan and leads the Linux download with the deb.
